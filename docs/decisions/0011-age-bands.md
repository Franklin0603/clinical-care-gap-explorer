# ADR-0011 · Band ages by the measure, not by decade

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-09-10 |
| **Affects** | one chart |

## Context

The gap rate is stratified by age. Ten-year bands are the conventional default.

## Options considered

- **Decades** — 30s, 40s, 50s… conventional and arbitrary
- **The measure's own boundaries** — defensible by citation

## Decision

**18–44, 45–64, 65–75, 76+.**

## Consequences

HEDIS's diabetes measure applies to members aged 18–75 and stratifies 18–64 and
65–75, so three of the four boundaries come from the measure rather than from
tidiness.

The 76+ band sits outside the measure's age range entirely — 21 of 116 patients
— which is worth showing rather than hiding.

"Because the measure stratifies that way" is a better answer than "because it
looked right".
